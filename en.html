Sogon

Speak, and it lands at your cursor.

A macOS menu-bar app for dictation and meeting recording. It transcribes what you say, optionally cleans it up with an LLM, and puts it where your cursor was when you started.

v0.31.6 macOS 14+ Apple Silicon On-device speech recognition Signed and notarized

Dictation

Hold the global shortcut, speak, and let go. The text goes into the app and field you were in when you started recording.

Back to where you were

Sogon remembers where focus was when you started and returns there right before it types. Editors like Kiro and VS Code get their input focus back, and with the Automation permission it also returns to the Chrome tab and field or the iTerm2 session, including tmux windows and panes.

Keep talking, keep the order

You can start the next recording while the last one is still being corrected. Transcription and correction run at the same time, but text reaches your cursor in the order you spoke.

Nothing leaves your Mac by default

MLX Audio or WhisperKit handles speech recognition on your Mac. LLM correction defaults to None, and a cloud provider is used only if you pick one.

Correct without speaking

Type or paste into the ⌃⇧T window and Sogon corrects the text and puts it at your cursor. You can also dictate into that window, fix things by hand, and then submit.

Fix the terms it keeps getting wrong

Add a correction like 스텝 펑션 → Step Functions to the dictionary and it's replaced right after transcription, with or without an LLM. You can import 142 Cloud/IT corrections grouped into 18 topics.

Throw it away

Press ⌃⇧⌫ while recording or transcribing and nothing gets typed. esc isn't used for canceling, so other apps still receive it.

Selected text and your screen

Text that's already on screen can be translated or rewritten with one key.

Translate with ⌘C twice

Select text and press ⌘C twice quickly. Korean becomes English and English becomes Korean, shown next to the original. The default engine, Apple Translation, translates on this Mac. It's off by default because it watches every ⌘C.

The ⌃⇧C action grid

Correct, structure, translate, change case, send to Notes, or ask a CLI about the selected text with a single key. Rewrites are shown to you before anything is replaced.

⌃⇧V snippets

Save text you type over and over, such as an account number, an address, or an email signature, and insert it by name. Snippets go in exactly as saved.

Ask about your screen with ⌃⇧S

Capture part of the screen and say your question. Sogon pastes the image and then the corrected text into your agent or chat box. This one is off by default too.

Meeting recording

Separate from dictation, a session can record for up to 3 hours. By default it records first and transcribes after the meeting.

Transcribe after the meeting

Because the audio is kept, transcription doesn't slow your Mac down during the call. When a better model comes along, you can transcribe the same audio again.

Minutes and a verbatim transcript

From one session you can create minutes with a summary, decisions, and action items, and a verbatim transcript with nothing summarized. Export either as .txt or .md, or send it to Apple Notes.

Your mic and system audio, separately

Your voice and the other side of the call are recorded on different tracks. When both are captured, the transcript is labeled [마이크] (mic) and [시스템 오디오] (system audio).

Who said what

On macOS 26 or later Sogon can separate speakers on your Mac, and with AWS Transcribe turned on it tells up to 30 people apart. You can name each speaker.

Recordings you already have

Import up to 20 files at a time in 9 formats, including mp3, m4a, wav, mp4, and mov, and they're transcribed the same way as a meeting session.

Dictate while it records

Press ⌃⇧D during a session to add dictation. The meeting keeps recording and only what you dictate goes to your cursor.

Install

This is a public release. Download it directly — no sign-in and no extra tooling.

Download Sogon.dmg from the v0.31.6 release, open it, and move Sogon.app to /Applications. The build is signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple, so it opens without a Gatekeeper warning. The user guide walks you through permissions and your first dictation.

With Homebrew: brew install --cask ddiiwoong/sogon/sogon. Sogon updates itself after that, so you do not need brew upgrade.

During a meeting recording, Stop Recording in the quick controls stops the session. Press ⌃⇧D again to stop only the dictation. You can't start a session while dictating, so start the session first.

Engines

You can switch transcription and correction engines without restarting the app.

StageOptions
Transcription (STT)MLX Audio (local) · WhisperKit (local, CoreML/ANE) · Groq Cloud
Correction and translation (LLM)None (default) · Amazon Bedrock (Nova, Claude) · Anthropic Claude · Google Gemini · OpenAI · Groq · Local MLX
Correction modeStandard · Filler Removal · Structured · Custom (prompt template)
Meeting speakersOn your Mac (macOS 26+) · AWS Transcribe (optional)

In Standard and Filler Removal modes, if a correction changes more than half the words, Sogon inserts the original instead. That keeps the model from summarizing your dictation or turning it into something else.
